Data Poisoning Attacks

Abstract

This repository presents an academic study of data poisoning attacks in machine learning systems. The research investigates training-time adversarial manipulations, their formal threat models, practical implementation, and impact on the security and robustness of machine learning models.

⸻

Index Terms

Data Poisoning Attacks, Adversarial Machine Learning, Machine Learning Security, Training-Time Attacks, Model Robustness.

⸻

Research Objectives

The objectives of this research are:
	•	to formally define data poisoning attacks and their threat models;
	•	to analyze existing attack strategies and taxonomies;
	•	to evaluate the effect of poisoned training data on model performance;
	•	to reproduce selected poisoning attacks through controlled experiments;
	•	to review and compare existing defense and mitigation techniques.

⸻

Scope

This research focuses on:
	•	supervised learning under adversarial training conditions;
	•	label-flipping and clean-label poisoning attacks;
	•	targeted and untargeted poisoning scenarios;
	•	backdoor injection during the training phase;
	•	security evaluation and robustness analysis of ML models.

⸻

Methodology

The research methodology includes:
	•	systematic review of peer-reviewed literature;
	•	formal analysis of attack mechanisms;
	•	practical implementation of poisoning techniques;
	•	empirical evaluation using quantitative metrics;
	•	comparative analysis of defense strategies.

⸻

Repository Organization

The repository is organized to support academic reproducibility and clarity.
Each research topic may include:
	•	references to primary academic sources;
	•	theoretical notes and definitions;
	•	experimental implementations and datasets;
	•	results, evaluation metrics, and observations.

⸻

Ethical Considerations

All experiments are conducted in controlled environments for academic and educational purposes only.
The materials are intended to improve understanding of machine learning security and support the development of defensive techniques.

⸻

Project Status

This repository represents an ongoing research project.
The content may be extended or revised as new findings are obtained.
